# THE 5,555 — a canon history

**Why this file exists.** The number has changed job eight times, in four repositories, over ten months, and
no single document ever carried the whole story — so reading any one of them leaves you with a coherent
piece of an incoherent whole. This is the reconstruction, verified against git history and the shipped
product by two independent review passes.

**Status: HISTORY, not canon.** `CANON.md` §1.2 / §1.2.1 / §1.2.2 is the ruling text. Where this file and `CANON.md`
disagree, `CANON.md` wins. This file records *how we got there*, what was tried, and what is still open.

---

## The one-paragraph answer

**5,555 is an arithmetic artifact from Comic Book 1** — the vial-count of a DNA-collection errand — that
was retrofitted into a cosmology as the Shattering's fragment count, and has since acquired eight successive
readings. It has **never once been implemented as a counter in any build**, and the system that would count
it is currently out of scope. What is settled: it counts **forms held simultaneously** — souls, not threads, and not a lifetime tally
(Phase 7, 2026-07-29) — and a fragment is **not divided by living more than one life**, so repeat bonding is
unlimited and permanent and advances nothing (Phase 8, 2026-07-30). What is unsettled is listed at the end, honestly.

## Phase −1 · The comic (pre-repo, before 2025-12-19)

The number's literal origin, stated in the lore's own founding synthesis under the heading
**"The Math (Comic Book 1 Origin)"** (`MASTER_LORE_PROPOSAL_FINAL.md:927-928`):

> *"Stoner Nike asked for **5,548 vials** to collect DNA from other Nikes across the multiverse. Add the 4
> Scientist Nikes, the 5 Champions, Stoner Nike himself, and Nike Prime — the number was always approaching
> 5,555."*

Corroborated in the data (`characters/comic-characters.json`, `timeline/prime-era.json`: *"Samples from
5,548 Nike variants gathered and integrated"*).

**⚠ That derivation is WRONG and is now superseded.** 5,548 + 4 + 5 + 1 + 1 = **5,559**, overshooting by four.
It was the corpus's only published justification for the number, it never closed, and nobody noticed for ten
months. **It is retained above as provenance only — never cite it.**

**✅ RESOLVED — owner ruling, 2026-07-29.** The number closes by subtraction, not addition:

```
5,555   all Nikes                       ← the owner's number; the comic never states it
   −1   Stoner Nike himself             ← he collects from "all of the Other Nike's" (script.md:339)
─────
5,554   others to collect from
   −6   already accounted for           ← Engineer · Computer Coder · Botanist · Nutritionist
─────                                     · Gladiator · Boxer
5,548   vials ordered                   ← the comic's one stated figure ✓
```

**"Already accounted for in the order," not "already swabbed."** Stoner Nike places the order at `script.md:339`
*before he has recruited anyone* — it is a planning figure that nets out the six already committed to the
project: the four Scholars who would build the chamber, Gladiator whose world is the base of operations, and
Boxer, a public champion. The spit vials handed to champions on-page (`:378, :508, :645, :675, :709`) come from
that same box; a vial is fungible, and it is the **order size** that excludes the six, not the physical glass.

**The 5,548 is the comic's; the 5,555 is the owner's.** `grep -c "5555\|5,555" comic-books/book-01/script.md`
returns **0**. No future document may cite Book 1 for the total — cite it for 5,548 and cite the owner for 5,555.

**The comic is the highest-authority *source* in the project** (`CONTRIBUTING.md` Tier 1 — under only the
owner's own ruling at Tier 0), **and for the first ten months it existed in no repository.** Every reading
below was therefore derived from a secondhand paraphrase of a document nobody working on it could open.

**Book 1 has since landed** — `comic-books/book-01/` — and it says **5,548 vials**, not 5,555
(`script.md:339`). What it settles and what it overturns is a live reconciliation, and per Tier 0 every
collision is a question put to the owner rather than an automatic rewrite. Comics 2–8 remain planned
titles only. Read this file's phases knowing the primary source arrived *after* all of them.

**Two incompatible referents existed from day one.** The oldest tracked commit uses 5,555 for **dimensions**
*and* for **souls**, in the same tree:

- `commands/campaign.js:232` — *"Nolem … hunts Nike souls across **5,555 dimensions**."*
- `scripts/master/part1_master.md:41` — *"There are **5,555 unique Nike souls** scattered across infinite realities."*

The dimensions reading is **still live in shipped code today** (`systems/raids/RaidBossConfig.js:2478`)
against `CANON.md` §1.3's **eight** dimensions.

*(Project artifacts date development to at least 2025-10-17 — backups, creature exports — but none of them
contains the number, so they date the project, not the number.)*

## Phase 3 · 2026-06-16/17 — the scaling fix

**The owner flags that a lifetime counter scales backwards.** Recomputed from the doc's own
`T = 5555/(P·b)` and both figures reproduce exactly:

- **Megaserver (P≈10,000, b=0.5): T ≈ 1.11 h.** The collective climax fires on the launch afternoon and,
  because a lifetime counter crosses 5,555 exactly once, **never again.**
- **Dying realm (P≈30): T ≈ 370 h**, rising as players leave. A new player sees a 4%-full sky.

A 3-mind consensus produces **`PATTERN_INTEGRITY.md`** (first git evidence 2026-06-17).

**Its own first draft was retracted, on two grounds.** It had framed 5,555 as *"a **constant**, not a
counter — nothing players do fills it"*, which overturned canon; and it was titled `THE_VIGIL.md`, colliding
with Comic Book 2's title, the post-war era in `timeline/ages.json`, and OG Nike's 3,000-year watch.
Renamed, and *"Vigil"* was flagged permanently off-limits. **The v1 artifacts are unrecoverable** — the
commits its own log cites are unknown revisions today.

**A deliberate non-sweep, worth recording:** review had measured ~40 canon-correct *"counts toward 5,555"*
passages against the v1 error. The ruling was that **the bible was right and the v1 edit was the outlier**,
so the fix collapsed to four edits. Surviving *"counts toward"* lines are **not** unswept residue.

**What landed:** **Pattern Integrity `I ∈ [0,1]`** — a *held* threshold against an always-on
**population-scaled decay**, `decay(P,W) = d0 + d1·P^α·W·surge(t)`, α default 0.75. ***"Popularity grows the
Devourer, never the ease."*** It **oscillates**; reach-and-hold **wins a chapter** and the war reopens. The
personal give-the-5,555th-bond climax is **phased, always reachable, gated on campaign completion** — never
on a personal count. Precedent cited and verified in shipped code: the Discord **`RiftMeter`** is cyclic
(`resetMeter()` zeroes it), not a lifetime tally.

**The doc marks itself `not locked`** — shape firm, constants are owner knobs. It still does.

## Phase 4 · 2026-07-18 — individuals, not species

The Tesana build prompt introduces the sentence that decides what the 5,555 *are*:

> ***"Two wild Nikes of the same species are distinct souls among the 5,555"*** — bonding a second of the
> same species is legal.

Live today at `GAME_SPEC.md:194` and `:1639`, and cited as canon by the co-op design. **Under this rule the
5,555 cannot be a species count**: one species yields many members of the 5,555. It is also what makes
Circle Draws legal — additional *distinct souls* can arrive without instancing anyone.

> **⛔ STRUCK — owner ruling, 2026-07-29. The 5,555 IS a species count.** The paragraph above is preserved as
> the record of what the individuals reading implied, not as a live constraint. The owner has ruled that
> **5,555 counts distinct Nike forms** — the original vision, and the reading `LORE_BIBLE.md` §9 carried before
> it was deleted without rationale (Phase 1 below). Bonding a second of the same form stays **legal**; it
> simply does not advance the 5,555 — and, since **2026-07-30**, repeat bonding is **unlimited and permanent**
> (§1.2.2, Phase 8). **Circle Draws, which were designed on the sentence above, are RE-FOUNDED and no longer
> await a ruling:** they needed a drawn soul to be *a distinct person*, and *a distinct **life*** supplies it.

## Phase 7 · 2026-07-29 — the referent: **forms**

Comic Book 1 lands in-repo and the owner rules Tier 0 above it. **The 5,555 counts distinct Nike FORMS, not
distinct bodies** — one form per universe, each of the 5,555 at once a unique individual *and* a unique kind,

Same session: the arithmetic closes by subtraction (`5,555 − 1 Stoner Nike − 6 already accounted for = 5,548
vials`), the **Bond Log is ruled COMPLETABLE**, and **NOTHING IS CONSUMED** settles that Nike Prime is *held,
not fuelled*.

## Phase 8 · 2026-07-30 — **one soul, many lives**

*"Bonding in my game works the same way as catching a pokemon… store all extras in the sanctuary… even if 5555
is complete, we can continue to bond with as many repeat species as we want."* **Unlimited repeat bonding
becomes canon and permanent** (§1.2.2), and Phase 7's loose end is tied off.

- **Derived, not added.** §1.2.1 already ruled a soul *"is not divided"* by being reached by many Collectors,
  *because distance and number are nothing to the Pattern.* The same sentence covers a soul living in more than
  one place at once. **A fragment is not divided by living more than one life.**
- **Two objects, one counted.** The **fragment** (a form — 5,555, counted by the Log and the Pattern) and the
  **life** (own name, nature, wound, memory of you — unbounded, counted by *nothing*).
- **Repeats are required by nothing** — no gate, counter or reward. ⚠ The stronger form first written here,
  *"a thing that advances no counter can never become a required grind — arithmetic, not tuning"*, was
  **struck the same day**: counters do not drive grind in this genre, **quality re-rolls** do, and three
  already exist (per-life IVs/natures, evolution-line coverage, the Resonant's catch-streak odds). What IS
  structural is the *realm-meter* immunity — the census is a **set of form-ids**, so a repeat of a
  **currently held** form is a no-op by data shape. (Evolve your only Sonic and you no longer hold Sonic;
  a repeat then does move the meter, correctly.)
- **The number finally has a post-completion answer: A CENSUS IS NOT A RESCUE.** The Log records that you have
  *met* all 5,555 — never that everyone is out of the dark. *You finish the collecting; you never finish the
  keeping; you never finish the reaching.*

## What is still open — verified, not speculative

1. **⛔ The number has never been implemented, and the system that would count it is CUT.** No `5555` appears
   in any `.js` file of the shipped game. The current build lists *"Pattern Integrity and the Pattern-sky"*
   under **"OUT — explicitly, and do not smuggle them back"**, calling it *"the most cuttable system in the
   whole spec."* **The history of 5,555 is the history of a number that is, to this day, a word in dialogue.**
2. **`PATTERN_INTEGRITY.md` is self-declared `not locked`** — yet `CANON_MAP.md` cites it as a DURABLE locked
   rule. A locked row sourced to an unlocked doc.
3. **The Bond Log's two definitions are incompatible.** It is sized at **5,555 slots** with two structurally
   unfillable (the player; the Nike Who Refuses) — *and* its unit is defined as *"every bond **given**"*, an
   event count that also logs frees, returns and severs and awards **+1 to every Circle member for the same
   soul**. A fixed-slot dex and a monotonic event log cannot be one object. **Off-by-one:** if the Refuser's
   slot is permanently empty, a completionist maxes at **5,553**, not the 5,554 the docs state.
   > ⛔ **MOOT as of 2026-07-29.** The owner ruled the Bond Log **completable** — no slot is permanently empty, so there is no off-by-one to resolve. The 5,555th-slot beat survives as the **campaign climax** (gated on campaign completion, never a count), and the Nike Who Refuses became an **arc** rather than an exemption.
4. **Integrity has two incompatible formalisations.** One section defines it as a **count of distinct souls
   currently connected** (a set capped at 5,555); another models it as a **rate equilibrium**. With a large
   population holding many bonds over at most 5,555 souls, the set reading pins the meter at 1.0 permanently
   and the flow reading does not.

## The species question — the record

**Canon commits to individual souls, not species**, explicitly and repeatedly: *"two wild Nikes of the same
species are distinct souls among the 5,555"* (`GAME_SPEC.md:194`, `:1639`); *"one soul is already in 5,555
**bodies**"* (`CANON.md` §1.2.1); *"there are exactly 5,555 **fragments**"* (§1.2); *"5,555 **individuals**,
each with their own story"* (shipped, `void_terminus/main_006_quiet_moment.json:57`). The implemented roster
is **292 species**, and the corpus says so: *"the lore's '5,555' is the aspirational soul-count; the
implemented roster is the numbers below."*

**But the species reading was the ORIGINAL reading, and it was never overruled — it was dropped.**

`LORE_BIBLE.md` §9, *"THE NIKE VARIANTS COMPENDIUM"* (deleted 2026-02-11, 19:00:06):

> ***"Expand the lore on all 5,555 Nike types"*** — followed by a taxonomy (Champion / Scholar / Oddity /
> Elemental / Lost), and *"Nike Prime can consciously access all 5,555."*

And in the same file: *"OG Nike fragmented into 5,555 pieces — **each one becoming a Nike in a different
dimension**"* — one kind per world.

The number's own origin is variant-flavoured too: the comic's *"DNA from 5,548 Nike **variants**."*

**There is no record of the species reading being proposed, debated, adopted or rejected.** `LORE_BIBLE.md`
was removed in a bare deletion commit with no rationale, alongside two other files, with no `CHANGELOG`
entry, no `GAME_DELTAS` row and no `CANON_MAP` row. **The individuals reading hardened around the gap five
months later.**

**So: adopting 5,555-as-species would be a return, not a reversal.** What it would cost is one ratified
sentence — *"two wild Nikes of the same species are distinct souls"* — which is load-bearing for Circle
Draws and would need a successor, in all three artifacts, in the same session.

> **✅ RESOLVED — and the predicted cost was paid exactly as predicted.** The owner ruled the **form**
> referent on **2026-07-29** (`CANON.md` §1.2, ruling 2), which struck that sentence and left the Circle
> Draws standing on nothing for one day. The successor landed on **2026-07-30**: **ONE SOUL, MANY LIVES**
> (`CANON.md` §1.2.2). A drawn soul needed to be *a distinct person*; it is now *a distinct **life***, and
> the Draws are re-founded (`Nikeverse-mmo-rpg-from-scratch/docs/CIRCLE_DESIGN.md` §5.3). **Both readings
> above survive rather than one beating the other:** *"one soul in 5,555 bodies"*, *"5,555 fragments"* and
> *"all 5,555 Nike types"* are all true at once, because a fragment is **at once a unique individual and a
> unique kind** — and, since 07-30, one that may be living any number of lives without being divided.
