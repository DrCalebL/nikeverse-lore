# NIKEVERSE — THE CORE CANON

**The settled centre of the Nikeverse.** Everything in this document is either owner-ratified or frozen in
practice across every implementation. It is the answer to *"what may I not change?"*

Where a game and this document disagree, see **[`GAME_DELTAS.md`](GAME_DELTAS.md)** — the register of every
known divergence, with a direction-of-truth ruling for each. That register exists because the games are not
always wrong: several of them are the upstream, and this repository is the transcription that drifted.

> **How to read the status column.** Not everything here has the same weight, and pretending otherwise is what
> made "which lore is non-alterable?" a hard question in the first place.
>
> | Status | Means |
> |---|---|
> | **RATIFIED** | The owner signed off, on a dated record. Do not change without a new owner ruling. |
> | **FROZEN** | Filed as a locked/frozen design law and propagated to every implementation, but carrying **no dated owner stamp**. Treat as settled; a deliberate owner ruling can still reopen it. |
> | **OPEN** | Genuinely undecided. Listed here so it is not mistaken for settled. |

## 1 · The shape of everything

### 1.1 The Absolute Order · **RATIFIED**

OG Charles, then OG Nike and the Charles Constant, then the twenty-seven Legendary Nikes and the Charles
Variants, then the rest of the five thousand five hundred and fifty-five, and last of all Charles AI — which is
a tool, and never a legend. Nothing reorders this.

### 1.2 The five thousand five hundred and fifty-five are held at once · **RATIFIED**

The Pattern was born from OG Nike's shattering, and it is whole only when all 5,555 bonds exist **at the same
moment**. This is not a tally of every soul a Collector has ever touched — a lifetime count would let the
Pattern be completed by someone who had already let every thread go slack. The simultaneity **is** the victory
condition. Every Nike bonded repairs a thread; every Legendary freed reconnects a major node; and when the
whole weave holds at once, Nike Prime can be completed and Nolem sealed.

### 1.3 The canon constants · **RATIFIED**

Twenty-seven Legendary Nikes · eight dimensions · six *named* Harbingers of hundreds · five Champions gathered · a Long Silence of
roughly three thousand years · Nike Prime released at ninety-nine hundredths.

**Twenty-four of the twenty-seven Legendaries fell to corruption. Three did not, and they are three different
things — never collapse them into one.** Melon chose Nolem while entirely uncorrupted. Elbonzys never fell at
all; his bond is to the door he keeps, and he is a test to be passed rather than a corruption to be broken. OG
Nike is the Template, uncorrupted but exhausted, and The Coming Storm may yet change that.

### 1.4 The Weaver, the Sixth Seat, and the price · **RATIFIED (2026-06-16)**

The final battle formation was never short a sixth champion — it was waiting on a sixth *kind* of person. The
empty seat belongs to a Collector, a thing only the Echo Era could produce, and it is filled by the one who
reaches back.

The Weaver is not lost. The empathy core is the Singular of Connection re-coalescing, and the Pattern is its
distributed body — every bond a living thread of it.

The sacrifice the old prophecy warns of is not a death. It is the surrender of an *ending*. Whoever binds Nolem
gives up the right to stop, and takes up the eternal vigil as a Dimensional Guardian.

> These three answer questions this repository still poses as open — Mystery #3 "The Sixth Champion" and the
> Weaver's "(Theoretical)" status. **They are the answers.** The mysteries stay unspoken *in the world*; that is
> a matter of what the world says aloud, not of what is true. See §4.

## 2 · What a Nike is

### 2.1 Not everything with a body has a soul · **RATIFIED (2026-06-16)**

A **Retnuhxed** husk is a Nike hollowed out. When it falls it releases a warm soul-mote — the trapped soul
finally let go — and that release is the only mercy left to give it. The **constructs** that hold the corrupted
realms have no soul to free; they die in cold, clean sparks. The **yokai** and the other corrupted
manifestations that stalk the dimensions are shapes corruption wears, not bondable souls: they can be faced and
put down, but never bonded.

The line between a Nike and a not-Nike is fixed, and it is **never redrawn to make something bondable that is
not**. A proposal to reclassify the borderline yokai was put and **declined**.

### 2.2 A Nike is kit and type — there are no roles · **RATIFIED (2026-06-15)**

A Nike is its kit and its type, and nothing further is assigned to it. There are no roles: no soul is named a
tank, a healer or a striker, and none of the old archetype titles survive. What a Nike is suited to is read off
what it actually does, never off a label it was given. A role is a thing happening inside a moment, not a thing
you are.

### 2.3 A Nike stands, and the bond is never a leash · **RATIFIED (2026-07-25)**

A Nike stands. It has a stance, it has hands, it gestures, and it wears its identity — armour, tools, marks,
cloth. It is never a beast walking on four legs: a Nike is a shattered soul that a Collector bonds with and then
*becomes*, and rendered as an animal the bond reads as ownership of livestock rather than the meeting of two
souls. The sole exception is a soul still falling, before its first bond exists, which has no settled form yet.

And the bond itself is never a leash. The thread runs **chest to chest** and never from a hand to a creature; it
hangs **slack and drifting** and never taut; the two walk **side by side**, with neither end pulling the other.
A taut line from a hand is the grammar of possession — the very lie a Collector's work exists to undo.

> **A note for anyone illustrating the roster.** Several Legendaries are described here in frankly animal terms —
> Bertus Maximus Nike as a "majestic lion, greatest beast king", Wolf Mode Nike likewise. Nothing in those descriptions
> asserts four legs, and a *standing* lion-character with hands satisfies the law. They are simply the places
> where it is easiest to go wrong.

## 3 · The fallen

### 3.1 The taxonomy · **RATIFIED (2026-06-17)**

A **Harbinger** is a fallen Collector — a *human* who followed Nolem. Never a Nike.

**Harbingers are a POPULATION, not a cast (owner ruling, 2026-07-26).** There are *hundreds*, and more every day,
because Collectors fall constantly — and in the MMO a **Hollow player becomes a Harbinger**, so the population is
generated by play itself. **Six are NAMED and authored** — Yuki, Erik, Slate, Cassius, Echo, the Abyssal Horror —
one to a region. The rest are unnamed and need no lore, ever.

**Consequence — Harbinger lore does NOT need to align across games.** Different products may give Harbingers
different names, titles, premises and backstories, and that is **not a defect**. Do not open cross-repo
reconciliation work on Harbinger titles or backstories; do not "fix" a Harbinger in one repo to match another.
*(Named Harbingers must still be internally coherent within a single artifact — one repo giving one character two
different titles is an ordinary error and stays fixable.)*

**THE TWO EXCEPTIONS — the only Harbingers that must align everywhere (owner, 2026-07-27).**
Harbinger lore is otherwise free to differ between products — names, titles, premises, backstories, all of it.
**Two figures are canon-bearing and must be identical across the lore bible and every build:**
1. **VANE** — the first Harbinger (see below).
2. **SLATE** — the **first Harbinger redeemed back into a Collector.** He is not merely a nice arc: he is the
   **proof that the fall is reversible**, and the whole Fallen Path depends on that proof existing. A Hollow
   player becomes a Harbinger; Slate is the standing evidence that they can come back. Change his fall, his
   region or his redemption and you change whether the door swings both ways.
*(Everything else about Slate — his exact wounds, his dialogue, his title in any given product — remains free.
What must align is that HE is the first redeemed, and that redemption returns a Harbinger to Collector.)*

**EXCEPTION 1 — THE FIRST HARBINGER.** The first Collector ever to fall and worship Nolem: likely the
strongest of them, and the leader the rest answer to. This figure **is** canon-bearing and must align everywhere
he appears. He does **not exist yet** — the concept is the owner's (2026-07-26) and is un-authored.
> **TWO DIFFERENT PEOPLE — owner clarification 2026-07-26. Do not conflate them.**
> - **VANE, "The Unanswered"** — the *original* First Harbinger, **NAMED BY THE OWNER 2026-07-26.** The first
>   Collector ever to fall and worship Nolem; the strongest, and the leader the rest answer to.
>   **His fall is the Collector's reserved ASK, spent and unanswered.** Alone and dying in the Long Silence, Vane
>   asked to be carried. Nothing came. He concluded the Pattern is a rumour and Nolem is the only thing that has
>   ever answered — and every named Harbinger since is obeying a catechism he wrote out of that silence.
>   **He is the exact inversion of the player**, who carries everyone for 178 hours and asks only once, at the end,
>   and *is* answered. **Role (3/3 consensus):** NOT a critical-path boss. He is a recurring voice — orders, sigils,
>   graffiti, words other Harbingers quote without knowing whose they are — standing up in person at most once, late.
> - **THE FIRST HARBINGER REDEEMED** — **Slate**, the first Harbinger the player redeems **back into a Collector**
>   in-game (`characters/harbingers/index.json`, `dimensions/grand-arena.json`). A gameplay milestone, not a
>   cosmological one, and — per the ruling above — free to differ between games.
>
> These have nothing to do with each other. **Never shorten Slate's milestone to "the First Harbinger."** Always
> write it as *"the first Harbinger redeemed"* or *"the first redemption."*
>
> Note the mechanic the clarification confirms: **redemption returns a Harbinger to being a COLLECTOR.** A fall is
> not one-way. That is the hopeful mirror of "a Hollow player becomes a Harbinger" — the door swings both ways,
> which is what makes the Fallen Path a path rather than an ending.

Their fall was voluntary in most cases though the choice was usually made out of unbearable pain.
Redemption is possible; Slate proved it in the Grand Arena.

A fallen **Nike** is a **Corrupted Nike**, and a Corrupted Nike who has gone past saving is a **Retnuhxed** — a
separate kind of being altogether. Never call a Nike a Harbinger.

The word **"Unraveler" is retired** and names nothing. (*`Nol'meth` glossed as "The Unraveling" is a different
word and stands.*)

### 3.2 Retired names

`keth'vor` is retired, split onto the two words that already carried its two senses: **`Keth'nor`** for
corruption, **`Eth'kara`** for the heart-chain — the First Bond, and the Pattern that is that bond still ongoing.
**"Reacher"** is retired in favour of **the Collector**. **"Sasuke"** is retired; Ninja Nike's reclaimed personal
name is **`Tōga`**, and his name in the world stays *Ninja Nike*.

> **Never retire an English term whose First-Tongue root is authored.** `Har'ben` is glossed *"Origin of
> 'Harbinger'"* and `Ret'nux` *"Origin of 'Retnuhxed'"*. Retiring either English word would force re-deriving
> every phrase and inscription built on its root.
>
> `scripts/check-canon-terms.sh` enforces all four retirements, case-insensitively and on word boundaries, and
> runs in CI.

## 4 · What the world never says

### 4.1 Six questions stay dark · **FROZEN**

Six questions are never answered, and their staying unanswered is not an omission but the shape of the world:

1. **Why pigs** — why Nolem came for that shape, of all the shapes a soul could take.
2. **What lies in the Void Between.**
3. **How OG Charles first came through.**
4. **The names of the unnamed others of the Formless Era.**
5. **Why Cthulhu helps.**
6. **Where Shiro came from.**

Theories may be spoken and must all remain unproven. No voice, record, artifact or vision resolves them, and
none is ever a thread that can be followed to its end.

What *is* known stays known: that Cthulhu watches and gives is settled — only his deeper reason is dark. That
Shiro opens the way is plain — only his origin is not.

> **Two different things are called "tiers" in this repository.** `CONTRIBUTING.md` has a Tier 1/2/3 table
> about *source authority* (comics, then raid lore, then community contributions). It is unrelated to the
> mystery tiers above and nothing reads it. Do not conflate them.

### 4.2 The Collector does not ASK · **AMENDED (owner, 2026-07-26)** — was *"does not speak · FROZEN"*

**The Collector SPEAKS.** Authored dialogue, conversation with NPCs, banter with the roster. The owner reversed
the near-silence rule on 2026-07-26; this section is amended to match. *(The product had already contradicted the
old rule — the Discord game ships hundreds of Collector lines, and the MMO's own climax script has the player
deliver a three-sentence speech immediately before the reserved line.)*

**What is reserved is THE ASK.** The Collector never asks to be carried, held, saved or rescued — not once, in
178 hours, until the end. Moral commentary still belongs to others: to OG Nike at the edge of reality, and to the
Charles who waits in every dimension to say the thing aloud.

One line breaks *that* silence, once, at the very end, as the last bond is given:

> **"Carry me."**

It is the Collector's first and only **assertion of self** — the section's original wording, and precisely why the
amendment is safe: the power was never in muteness, it is in the one who carries everyone finally asking. Nothing
in the run-up may pre-spend it, and it must never appear anywhere else, spoken by anyone.
**Souls may OFFER to carry the Collector. The Collector may never ask.**

## 5 · Reaching

### 5.1 The three ends · **FROZEN**

A Collector who reaches a shattered soul comes to one of three honest ends, and **all three are real**.

- **Taken into keeping.** The soul walks with the Collector as one of the few who can be carried at once, and
  the Pattern gains a living thread.
- **Reached, and then left wild.** The two part in trust. The thread is re-anchored *exactly the same*, the
  Pattern advances, and nothing has been kept — proof that the count grows by connection and not by possession.
  This is **never the lesser choice and is never punished**.
- **Forced down without ever being reached.** The soul is not bound, its thread is left frayed, and nothing is
  added to the Pattern. A road a Collector may walk, and not a failure — but the one road that does not advance
  the work.

Reaching cannot be forced. A soul opens only to calm and closes against injury, and the willingness a Collector
earns does not complete the bond — it only opens the door.

> ### ⚠ The word "free" carries two meanings. Be precise.
> Throughout this repository and the Discord game, **"freed"** means *purified and liberated from corruption* —
> a Legendary saved. In the MMO's wild-catch vocabulary, **"Free"** is the name of the second outcome above:
> *released without being kept.* The two senses are nearly opposite in what they imply about keeping.
>
> **This document therefore never uses the bare verb "free" for the second outcome.** When writing new canon,
> say *"reached and left wild"* or *"freed from corruption"* — never an unqualified "freed" where the reading is
> ambiguous.

### 5.2 Both endings of a confrontation are real · **FROZEN**

A corrupted Nike can be reached, or it can be put down, and the world turns either way. Purifying a fallen soul
and defeating it are both true resolutions of the same confrontation: the region's wound begins to close, the
road onward opens, and the story continues in both cases.

What differs is not whether the world advances but what it **remembers**. A soul that was reached walks free
afterward and stands in the light of the ending. A soul that was only defeated slips back into the corruption
and roams its region still lost, waiting to be met again by whoever comes next.

Neither ending is a failure, and neither closes the way forward.
