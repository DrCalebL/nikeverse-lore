# NIKEVERSE — ARCHIVED CANON

Canon that an owner ruling has **superseded**, kept here **verbatim** with the ruling that replaced it, the date and
the reasoning — so a reader can always see what the canon *used to* say and *why* it moved. `CANON.md` carries only
the live canon; each archived passage leaves a one-line pointer in place (*"⤷ archived: ARCHIVE.md §A-n"*).

**How to use this file.** Never cite an archived passage as canon. Never delete an entry: an archive that loses its
history cannot explain the rule that replaced it (the same reason `CHANGELOG.md` keeps every ruling). New entries go
at the end, numbered `A-n`.

## A-1 · The head-start "never retreats" — unqualified (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`CANON.md` §5.3, the 2026-08-15 amendment):

> damage only ever **strips the numbing**, so it either shortens the reach (the head-start — a monotone meter FLOOR
> that rises as HP is driven down and never retreats, unfrozen owner 2026-08-15) or, overshot, KOs.

**Now:** the head-start **never retreats within an attempt.** An attempt ends when a fight is truly abandoned (or,
for a legendary, when the group wipes); the soul then returns home whole — its health regrown and its head-start
gone — and the next attempt starts fresh.

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-28/29 (build repo `docs/design/GAME_SPEC.md` §4.10, rulings C6 and G3).

**Why:** wild Nikes now stand up out of the grass and fight in the open, and legendaries must be epic battles that a
wiped group cannot simply chip down across retries. An unqualified "never retreats" let a player weaken a soul, walk
away, and come back to a banked head-start — or a group wipe and keep its progress. Inside one attempt nothing
changes: the floor still rises as health falls and never drops.

## A-2 · Wild aggression framed only as fighting "back" (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`CANON.md` §5.3, the 2026-08-02 extension — the owner's quote itself is **kept** there, verbatim):

> wild nikes should attack us back during encounter — the Nolem in them awakens and knows we want to cleanse them.

read as: a wild only fights once it is engaged.

What is archived here is that **reading**, not the quote: the "Was" is an interpretation of the owner's words, and
the quote itself is not superseded text — it stays live in `CANON.md` §5.3.

**Now:** a wild does not wait to be struck. The owner-approved line (2026-09-29): *"A wild does not wait to be
struck: the Nolem in it wakes when a Collector comes near, and it is the stain that moves first — never the soul."*
Wild Nikes notice a Collector at a distance (a visible notice beat first, and a presence cue you can sense from
further away than it can sense you), then attack.

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-29 (build repo `GAME_SPEC.md` §4.10.1, ruling 5b / G6).

**Why:** the owner's own 2026-08-02 words already said the Nolem in them *awakens and knows we want to cleanse them*
— waking on a Collector's approach is that sentence read literally. It keeps both laws intact: what attacks is the
corruption, never the soul, and the soul is never the aggressor.

## A-3 · The starter: "chooses a starter Nike" from three (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`characters/collectors-journey.json`, the Walker's opening):

> "Retnuhxed track the dimensional tear and breach the Hub perimeter",
> "Walker chooses a starter Nike (Piglet Nike, Emo Nike, or Chef Nike) and fights",

**Now:** **one soul fell with you.** The soul the Walker reached for in the fall lies dim beside them in the Hub; at
the Mural the Walker reaches for it, and it settles as Piglet, Emo or Chef — *"…you're the one. You're the one who
reached back."* Only then does the breach open and the Retnuhxed come through, and the Nike steps between the Walker
and the dark. The three are the shapes that one soul may take, never three souls in the world.

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-26 (opening-revamp spec §11 item 9: *"The 3 options are game options, not 3 actual souls
bonding. There was only 1 falling with me."*), built 2026-09-28 (build repo `GAME_SPEC.md` §5.4 "The choice").

**Why:** the fall is the Walker reaching back for one soul; three volunteers in the Hub made that reach meaningless.
The breach moves after the choice so the soul that fell with you is the one that stands between you and the dark.
(The settling is a witness, never a cause — `CANON.md` §1.2.3's law: *the bond is the witness, never the cause*.
Charles says the same thing at the Mural, in his own words — *"Reach for it — it will tell you who it is."* — which
is his line in the build's opening, not a quotation of §1.2.3.)

## A-5 · Tōga (Ninja Nike) fell in the Forgetting (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`timeline/long-silence.json`, `timeline/legendary-falls.json`): Ninja listed among The Forgetting's falls
(300–500 years after the Prime Era), *"Lost purpose - saw futures without hope"* / *"Saw futures without hope. Made a
contract to end the visions. The price was his freedom."*

**Now:** he fell **fifteen years before the Echo Era** (Genesis) — the last Champion to fall, after nearly three
thousand years of holding out — by a contract to protect his people from a Retnuhxed Apex (`characters/legendary-nikes`
already said so).

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-29 (timeline ruling 4).

**Why:** the character file and the timeline disagreed. Fifteen years lets Yuki have been his student and his clan
still remember him, and a Champion who held out almost the whole Silence is the stronger story.

## A-6 · Melon's fall era "Unknown" (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`characters/legendary-nikes/index.json`): `"corruptionEra": "Unknown"`; `timeline/legendary-falls.json`
filed him only under "Special Status".

**Now:** he chose Nolem — entirely uncorrupted — **in the Dark Age**, and with his choice fell the one great rising of
hope that age had. Still not a corruption (§1.3). His Prime-Era role and why he was not recruited stay Unknown.

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-29 (timeline ruling 6).

**Why:** it explains why hope was "nearly extinct" before the Convergence brought it back, and gives the Dark Age the
defining event it lacked. The fuller Melon story remains a proposal awaiting his Tier-A ruling.
