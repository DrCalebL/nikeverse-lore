# NIKEVERSE — ARCHIVED CANON

Canon that an owner ruling has **superseded**, kept here **verbatim** with the ruling that replaced it, the date and
the reasoning — so a reader can always see what the canon *used to* say and *why* it moved. `CANON.md` carries only
the live canon; each archived passage leaves a one-line pointer in place (*"⤷ archived: ARCHIVE.md §A-n"*).

**How to use this file.** Never cite an archived passage as canon. Never delete an entry: an archive that loses its
history cannot explain the rule that replaced it (the same reason `CHANGELOG.md` keeps every ruling). New entries go
at the end, numbered `A-n`.

## A-1 · The head-start "never retreats" — unqualified (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`CANON.md` §5.3, the 2026-08-15 amendment):

> damage only ever **strips the numbing**, so it either shortens the reach (the head-start — a monotone meter FLOOR
> that rises as HP is driven down and never retreats, unfrozen owner 2026-08-15) or, overshot, KOs.

**Now:** the head-start **never retreats within an attempt.** An attempt ends when a fight is truly abandoned (or,
for a legendary, when the group wipes); the soul then returns home whole — its health regrown and its head-start
gone — and the next attempt starts fresh.

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-28/29 (build repo `docs/design/GAME_SPEC.md` §4.10, rulings C6 and G3).

**Why:** wild Nikes now stand up out of the grass and fight in the open, and legendaries must be epic battles that a
wiped group cannot simply chip down across retries. An unqualified "never retreats" let a player weaken a soul, walk
away, and come back to a banked head-start — or a group wipe and keep its progress. Inside one attempt nothing
changes: the floor still rises as health falls and never drops.

## A-2 · Wild aggression framed only as fighting "back" (archived 2026-09-29)

**Was** (`CANON.md` §5.3, the 2026-08-02 extension — the owner's quote itself is **kept** there, verbatim):

struck: the Nolem in it wakes when a Collector comes near, and it is the stain that moves first — never the soul."*

**Ruling:** owner, 2026-09-29 (build repo `GAME_SPEC.md` §4.10.1, ruling 5b / G6).

**Why:** the owner's own 2026-08-02 words already said the Nolem in them *awakens and knows we want to cleanse them*
— waking on a Collector's approach is that sentence read literally. It keeps both laws intact: what attacks is the
corruption, never the soul, and the soul is never the aggressor.
